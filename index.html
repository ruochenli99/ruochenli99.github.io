Ruochen Li

Building MLLM as the brain of medical robots.

I develop multimodal large language models (MLLMs) as the intelligent brain of medical robots. My research asks how MLLMs can build genuine understanding from multimodal observations and use that understanding as the foundation for planning adaptive robot actions.

I am a PhD student at CAMP at the Technical University of Munich, supervised by Prof. Nassir Navab at TUM and Prof. Alison Noble at the University of Oxford, and mentored by Dr. Yuan Bi. My PhD is supported through the Hans Fischer Senior Fellowship at the TUM Institute for Advanced Study. I completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science at TUM with distinction.

ReEvalMed: Rethinking Medical Report Evaluation by Aligning Metrics with Real-World Clinical Judgment

Ruochen Li*, Jun Li*, Bailiang Jian, Kun Yuan, Youxiang Zhu

A clinician-validated meta-evaluation benchmark for radiology report metrics, revealing clinical misinterpretations and score-inflating practices in widely used metrics.

paperdataset

EMNLP 2026 Findings

Beyond Scalar Scores: Exploring LLM-based Metrics for Clinical Significance Evaluation in Radiology Reports

Qingyu Lu*, Ruochen Li*, ..., Dacheng Tao

Post-trained Qwen-8B and MedGemma-4B with SFT/DPO + LoRA, outperforming 32B medical LLMs on clinical alignment while exposing evaluator bias and robustness failures.

arXiv

Under review

Surgical Planning Evaluation for Video-LLMs

Ruochen Li*, Kun Yuan*, ..., Nassir Navab

Studies how to evaluate long-horizon surgical planning in safety-critical settings, separating visual grounding failures from planning failures and examining how structural knowledge helps constrain model behavior.

Under review

Training-free focus directions in key/query activations steer LLM attention toward task-relevant context, mitigating distraction across multiple LLM families.
